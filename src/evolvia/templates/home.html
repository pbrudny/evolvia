How it works

  1. Play. Sign in with GitHub and play the current version.
  2. Reach a milestone. That unlocks the "What if…?" button for this version.
  3. Suggest and vote. One idea per day. Vote for the ideas of others who played.
  4. Watch it evolve. The top idea is built by AI, checked by automatic tests and a review, and released as a new version with your name on it.
